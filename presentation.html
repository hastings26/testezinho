Module 04

The context window

One of the most misunderstood ideas in AI.

Think of one finite desk. Your prompt, the conversation, files, images and the system instructions all share the same surface.

Window = 200,000 tokensfilling…
Conversation 100k
PDF 80k
Sys 10k
Answer 10k
Conversation Uploaded PDF System prompt Room for the answer

⚠ Window full → old information is forgotten, compressed, or dropped. Not because the AI wants to — because there's literally nowhere left to store it.

The honest version

Teach principles, not habits

“ChatGPT is for brainstorming” is a beginner shortcut that ages badly. Here's the durable framing — and a demo that proves the point: show the same question answered four times, vote blind, then reveal the names.

ChatGPT

Strong all-rounder — writing, reasoning, coding and multimodal tasks.

Claude

Excellent for long documents, writing quality and sustained reasoning.

Gemini

Strong for research, multimodal understanding and the Google ecosystem.

Copilot

Best when work depends on your Microsoft 365 data — email, Teams, SharePoint, docs, workflows.

The lesson that lands: the model is one part of the equation. Task, context and available data often matter more than the logo.
Module 08

Chat vs Copilot vs CoWork

Each ring inherits everything inside it.

Ring 1

Chat

  • Knows only what you give it
  • Public knowledge only
  • Cannot see company files
Ring 2

Copilot Chat

+ everything in Chat
  • Your company identity
  • Microsoft Graph + permissions
  • Email · Calendar · Teams
  • SharePoint documents
Ring 3

CoWork

+ everything in Copilot
  • Agents
  • Business workflows
  • Automation
  • Higher-value tasks · credits
Now it's obvious why CoWork costs money — it does more, reaches further, and acts on your behalf.
