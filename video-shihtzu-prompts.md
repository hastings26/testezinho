# "Um dia comum, que é o nosso favorito": 8 prompts prontos (Veo 3 / Sora / Kling)

Cada prompt é autocontido: cole um por vez, 8 s cada, 16:9, e junte os 8 na edição (≈ 60 s).
Os modelos de vídeo respondem melhor em inglês, por isso os prompts estão em inglês.
Para o vídeo sair com o rosto de vocês, anexe em todos os planos as fotos de referência
(você, seu marido, cada shih tzu).

> Ajuste rápido: troque os trechos entre `[ ]` (cores do pelo, roupas, lugar) se quiser.

---

## Bloco fixo (já incluso em cada prompt abaixo)

```
STYLE: Live action, photorealistic, shot on a cinema camera with a 35mm lens at f/2.0, shallow
depth of field. Natural light only: soft window light or golden hour, no studio lighting.
Gentle handheld camera, slow organic movement. Warm, soft palette of cream, honey and olive
green. Subtle 35mm film grain. Intimate documentary short-film look. 16:9, 24 fps.
No on-screen text, no cartoon, no animation, no 3D/CGI look.

CAST: Two men in their late 30s, a married couple. Man A [short dark hair, light stubble,
beige knit sweater]. Man B [short brown hair, trimmed beard, light-blue linen shirt].
Two small shih tzus with realistic, detailed fur: Dog 1 [white and golden, puppy cut,
small pink bow]; Dog 2 [white and dark brown, slightly chubby, tongue always peeking out].
```

---

### 1. Manhã (0–8 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Close-up of the two shih tzus asleep, curled together on a white duvet. Soft morning
light filters through a linen curtain, dust particles glowing in the air. Dog 1 opens one
eye and yawns. The camera pushes in very slowly.
AUDIO: Birds outside, soft breathing, first notes of a gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar.
```

### 2. O despertar (8–16 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Medium shot in bed. Man A wakes up as both shih tzus jump on him, tails wagging,
licking his face. Man B, next to him, laughs with his eyes still closed and pulls the
blanket over his head. Spontaneous, chaotic, affectionate.
AUDIO: Laughter, happy little snorts from the dogs, rustling sheets, guitar continues.
```

### 3. Café (16–24 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Kitchen in strong side light from a window. Man B makes pour-over coffee; steam
visible against the window. Both shih tzus sit on the wooden floor looking up, waiting for
a piece of bread. Man A walks into frame and kisses Man B on the shoulder.
AUDIO: Coffee pouring, a cup on the counter, tiny paws tapping, soft piano joins the guitar.
```

### 4. Passeio (24–32 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Exterior, golden hour, [a tree-lined residential street]. Low camera at dog height:
little paws trotting, ears bouncing. The camera rises to reveal the couple holding hands,
each holding a leash. Warm backlight through the leaves.
AUDIO: Leaves, distant city sounds, footsteps, music swells slightly.
```

### 5. Brincadeira (32–40 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Green park lawn, sun behind the subjects, soft lens flare. Dog 1 chases a small ball
in slow motion, fur flowing. Dog 2 gives up halfway and rolls onto its back, belly up.
The couple laughs in the out-of-focus background.
AUDIO: A short happy bark, laughter, music at its warmest point.
```

### 6. Detalhes (40–48 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Three quick close-up detail shots: Man A's hand scratching behind a shih tzu's ear;
Man B's wedding ring as he gently holds a tiny paw; a wet nose sniffing the lens and
fogging it up.
AUDIO: Soft sniffing, a giggle, music continues.
```

### 7. Fim de tarde (48–56 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Living room in amber sunset light. The couple on the sofa under a knitted throw,
both shih tzus asleep between them. Man B rests his head on Man A's shoulder.
Static wide shot, calm and still.
AUDIO: Quiet room tone, a distant clock, music slows down.
```

### 8. Final (56–64 s)
```
[STYLE + CAST]
SHOT: Close-up of Dog 2's face sleeping, slow breathing, lit by a warm table lamp.
Hold, then a slow fade to black.
AUDIO: A single soft sigh from the dog, the last guitar note rings out.
```

---

## Montagem
- Ordem 1 → 8, cortes secos; cross-dissolve curto (0,5 s) só entre o 6 e o 7.
- Corte para ~7,5 s cada para fechar em 60 s.
- Se o modelo gerar música diferente em cada plano, tire o áudio dos clipes e coloque
  uma única trilha acústica (violão + piano, ~70 bpm) por baixo de tudo, mantendo só o som ambiente.
