The data mandate

Three rules, one card

The five kinds of data you handle
D0 PublicAnything on pax8.com or a public site
D1 InternalPlaybooks, training, anonymised aggregates
D2 Partner confidentialNamed pipeline, MDF, QBR content
D3 Partner end-customerTelemetry, billing IDs, named environments
D4 Regulated / restrictedGDPR special category, payment data, legal hold
1
Never paste D3 or D4 into an unapproved tool.
2
Use only approved tools for the data class you're holding.
3
Verify before you act. AI assists; humans decide.
OK — a QBR in M365 Copilot (T1) using pipeline (D2).   Not OK — that same pipeline pasted into chatgpt.com (T4).
"If this output were subtly wrong and I didn't notice — what would happen?"

If the answer scares you, stop. That question is the whole mandate in one line.
